# Pilot Launch Governance Pack

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft index

## 1. Purpose

Collect the evidence required for a formal pilot go-live decision.

## 2. Required Evidence

### Product / Scope
- approved pilot scope;
- approved specialty;
- selected facilities;
- current PRD/SRS;
- scope guardrails.

### Clinical
- approved case template;
- clinical workflow;
- priority policy;
- safety hazard log;
- governance lead;
- incident process.

### Legal / Privacy
- resolved blocker questions;
- executed agreements;
- data-processing position;
- hosting/data-residency approval;
- DPIA where required;
- retention schedule.

### Security / Technology
- selected platform architecture;
- threat model;
- security review;
- penetration test/independent review where feasible;
- access-control testing;
- backup restore evidence;
- monitoring readiness.

### Operations
- support model;
- operations runbook;
- escalation contacts;
- training completion;
- fallback workflow test.

### Evaluation
- evaluation protocol;
- minimum dataset;
- ethics determination;
- reporting validation.

## 3. Go-Live Meeting

Agenda:

1. unresolved blockers;
2. residual clinical risk;
3. residual security/privacy risk;
4. operational readiness;
5. legal/regulatory readiness;
6. consultant capacity;
7. facility readiness;
8. decision.

## 4. Possible Decisions

- GO
- GO WITH CONDITIONS
- DELAY
- NO-GO

## 5. Record

The final decision should record:

- date;
- participants;
- evidence reviewed;
- conditions;
- unresolved risks;
- accountable signatories.
