# Pilot Training and Onboarding Plan

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Objective

Users should understand both how to use the platform and what Sanad is clinically and operationally intended to be.

## 2. Training Audiences

- local doctors;
- consultants;
- coordinators;
- specialty leads;
- clinical governance users;
- administrators/support staff.

## 3. Common Training

All users should understand:

- provider-to-provider model;
- Sanad is not an emergency service;
- confidentiality;
- individual accounts;
- reporting security/privacy concerns;
- acceptable use;
- support channels.

## 4. Local Doctor Training

- create case;
- avoid unnecessary identifiers;
- consent confirmation;
- select routine/priority appropriately;
- attach useful files;
- submit under weak connectivity;
- respond to information request;
- interpret consultant advice as support to local judgement;
- record outcome;
- report incident/complaint.

## 5. Consultant Training

- availability/assignment;
- accept/decline;
- review structured case;
- request information;
- provide clear documented advice;
- record limitations;
- use addendum rather than overwrite;
- second opinion;
- incident reporting.

## 6. Coordinator Training

- routing;
- minimum necessary clinical access;
- priority handling;
- reassignment;
- escalation;
- outage/fallback process;
- handover.

## 7. Governance Training

- audit;
- incidents;
- complaints;
- second opinions;
- access restrictions;
- corrective actions;
- monthly review.

## 8. Administrator Training

- role changes;
- verification technical support;
- no default clinical browsing;
- secrets/security;
- audit obligations;
- production-access rules.

## 9. Training Format

Recommended:

- short live onboarding;
- mobile-friendly written guides;
- short videos if bandwidth permits;
- synthetic practice cases;
- role-specific quick reference;
- Arabic and English materials.

## 10. Competency Check

Before live access, high-risk roles should demonstrate:

- correct case workflow;
- privacy expectations;
- priority/emergency boundary;
- incident route;
- account-security basics.

## 11. Feedback

Collect:

- unclear workflow;
- missing terminology;
- time burden;
- connectivity difficulty;
- training gaps.

Training should be revised during the pilot based on actual user behaviour.
