# Legal and Regulatory Question Register

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Questions for qualified counsel and regulators, not legal conclusions

## 1. Purpose

Capture the legal and regulatory questions that must be answered before the pilot.

Engineering and product teams must not silently resolve these questions themselves.

## 2. Sudan: Professional Practice

Questions:

1. How is provider-to-provider tele-expertise classified under current Sudanese law/professional rules?
2. Is specific authorisation required from Sudan Medical Council?
3. Can consultants outside Sudan provide professional advice to a locally treating doctor?
4. What registration status must the remote consultant maintain?
5. Does the local treating doctor retain sole clinical responsibility, or can the remote consultant share liability?
6. Are there restrictions on advice across state boundaries?
7. What rules apply to clinical record retention?

## 3. Patient Consent

Questions:

1. What form of consent is required for remote specialist review?
2. Is verbal consent sufficient if documented by the treating doctor?
3. Are separate rules required for photographs, imaging, or teaching/research reuse?
4. What rules apply to minors or patients lacking capacity?

## 4. Cross-Border Consultant Practice

For each consultant home/practice jurisdiction:

1. Does their professional regulator permit advice to doctors abroad?
2. Does professional indemnity cover this activity?
3. Does employer policy restrict external clinical advice?
4. Are there country-specific telemedicine restrictions?

## 5. Data Protection

Questions:

1. Who is the data controller?
2. Are partner facilities independent or joint controllers?
3. Is a technology vendor a processor/subprocessor?
4. What lawful basis applies to clinical processing?
5. What special-category/health-data conditions apply?
6. What data-transfer rules apply when consultants access data from abroad?
7. Is a DPIA mandatory?
8. What breach notification obligations apply?
9. What retention periods are legally required?
10. What rights do data subjects have in this context?

## 6. Hosting and Data Residency

Questions:

1. Can clinical data be hosted outside Sudan?
2. Are specific countries/regions preferred or prohibited?
3. Do UK/EU rules apply if the organisation is established there?
4. Does remote consultant access constitute an international data transfer requiring safeguards?
5. Can backups reside in another region?

## 7. Research and Publication

Questions:

1. When does pilot evaluation become human-subject research?
2. Which ethics body should review the work?
3. Can operational data be reused for publication?
4. What consent or waiver is required?
5. Can cases be reused for teaching?
6. What de-identification standard is expected?

## 8. Organisation Structure

Questions:

1. Which legal structure best supports:
   - grants;
   - staff payment;
   - consultant payment;
   - liability;
   - future contracting?
2. Is a UK CIC still the preferred option after tax/accounting advice?
3. What governance duties apply to founders/directors?

## 9. Contracts

Required contract types may include:

- consultant services;
- facility MoU/data-sharing;
- employee/contractor;
- technology vendor;
- funder;
- research collaboration.

Questions:

- governing law;
- liability caps;
- indemnities;
- confidentiality;
- data protection;
- IP;
- termination;
- audit;
- data return/deletion.

## 10. Sanctions and Payments

Questions:

1. Which UK/US/EU/UN sanctions rules apply to:
   - partners;
   - facilities;
   - consultants;
   - payments;
   - banking?
2. Is screening required at onboarding and periodically?
3. Can in-country staff be paid through partner organisations/mobile banking?
4. Are there prohibited regions/entities?

## 11. Insurance

Questions:

- organisational professional liability;
- cyber insurance;
- directors/officers cover;
- clinical indemnity;
- cross-border coverage.

## 12. Priority Before Pilot

Classify each question:

- Blocker before platform/vendor selection
- Blocker before contracting
- Blocker before live clinical service
- Can be resolved during pilot preparation

## 13. Legal Advice Record

For each resolved question record:

- question ID;
- adviser;
- jurisdiction;
- date;
- written advice/reference;
- decision;
- affected documents/architecture;
- review date.

## Required Expert Review

**Level:** Blocking for the relevant decision gates.

**Experts required:**
- Healthcare Legal Counsel with Sudan and relevant cross-border expertise
- Privacy/Data Protection Counsel or DPO
- Sanctions/Compliance specialist
- Medical Indemnity / Insurance specialist
- Tax/Corporate adviser for entity and payment questions

The Project Manager should assign each question to the appropriate expert and record written advice in the Legal Advice Record section.
