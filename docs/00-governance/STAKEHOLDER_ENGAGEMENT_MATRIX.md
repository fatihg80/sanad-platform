# Stakeholder Engagement Matrix

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Active draft

## Engagement Levels

- **Manage Closely**: high influence, high impact
- **Keep Satisfied**: high influence, lower day-to-day involvement
- **Keep Informed**: highly affected but lower formal power
- **Monitor / Engage as Needed**

| Stakeholder | Influence | Impact | Engagement Level | When to Engage |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Clinical Governance Lead | High | High | Manage Closely | all clinical/safety decisions |
| Pilot Specialty Lead | High | High | Manage Closely | specialty, templates, safety, UAT |
| Local Doctors | Medium/High | High | Manage Closely | discovery, design, usability, pilot |
| Consultants Abroad | High | High | Manage Closely | capacity, workflow, indemnity, pilot |
| Facility Leadership | High | High | Manage Closely | selection, agreements, operations |
| Project Lead | High | High | Manage Closely | all stage gates |
| Technology Lead | High | High | Manage Closely | architecture, platform, delivery |
| Sudan Medical Council | High | High | Keep Satisfied / Consult | professional practice decisions |
| Federal/State Health Authorities | High | High | Keep Satisfied / Consult | pilot legitimacy and scale |
| Privacy/DPO | High | High | Manage Closely | data, hosting, DPIA, vendors |
| Legal Counsel | High | High | Manage Closely | liability, agreements, cross-border |
| Security Specialist | High | High | Manage Closely | architecture, vendor, go-live |
| NGO/Partner Organisation | High | Medium/High | Manage Closely | facility support, funding, logistics |
| Funders/Donors | High | Medium | Keep Satisfied | funding, milestones, evaluation |
| Patients | Low formal / High ethical | High | Protect / Represent | consent, privacy, safety design |
| Research/Evaluation Team | Medium | Medium/High | Keep Informed / Consult | metrics, ethics, publication |
| Platform Vendor | Medium/High | Medium/High | Manage Contractually | discovery, implementation, support |
| Cloud/Hosting Provider | Medium | Medium | Manage Contractually | hosting/security/data residency |

## Engagement Rule

Engagement should be driven by:

- decision authority;
- risk;
- impact;
- expertise;
- legal/regulatory role;
- operational dependency.

The loudest stakeholder should not automatically have the strongest decision right.
