# Expert Engagement Policy

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Active draft

## Purpose

Ensure Sanad does not allow product, technical, clinical, legal, privacy, research, or operational teams to make decisions outside their competence.

## Required Document Marker

Any document containing a decision that needs specialist validation should include a section named:

## Required Expert Review

with:

- **Expert role**
- **Questions to review**
- **Required before**
- **Decision owner**
- **Evidence to retain**

## Review Levels

### Advisory
Expert input improves quality but does not block progress.

### Required
Expert review must occur before the affected decision is approved.

### Blocking
Work must not proceed past the named gate without expert approval or documented risk acceptance by the accountable authority.

## Examples

### Legal
Engineering may describe a desired workflow, but cannot conclude that cross-border medical advice is legally permitted.

### Clinical
Product may propose priority categories, but cannot approve clinical triage criteria.

### Privacy
Technology may propose a hosting region, but cannot decide international transfer legality.

### Security
Developers may implement controls, but an independent security review should challenge the final system before go-live.

### Research
Product analytics may capture usage, but publication-oriented evaluation needs research/ethics review.

## Project Manager Responsibility

The Project Manager owns orchestration, not specialist judgment.

The PM must ensure:

- the right expert is engaged;
- review occurs at the right stage;
- evidence is captured;
- affected documents are updated;
- unresolved expert questions remain visible in registers.

## No Silent Approval

Absence of feedback is not approval.

If required expert input is missing, the decision remains Open or Proposed.
