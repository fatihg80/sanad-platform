# Stakeholder Register

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Active

## Purpose

Identify parties who influence Sanad, are affected by Sanad, or must be consulted because they hold authority, expertise, resources, or risk.

## Stakeholder Categories

### Primary Clinical Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Affected By Project | Influence | Main Interest | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local doctors inside Sudan | Very High | Very High | useful specialist support, low burden, safety | co-design, field testing, feedback |
| Specialist consultants abroad | High | Very High | safe workload, liability clarity, professional value | discovery, governance, pilot |
| Patients | Very High | Indirect/High | safety, confidentiality, consent | represented through clinical/privacy governance |
| Specialty Leads | High | Very High | clinical quality | continuous involvement |
| Clinical Governance Lead | High | Very High | safety, audit, incidents | decision authority |

### Operational Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duty Coordinators | High | workflow, routing, service continuity | workflow design/UAT |
| Facility Clinical Leaders | Very High | local adoption, safety | partner selection and governance |
| Facility IT / Admin | Medium/High | access, devices, connectivity | technical discovery |
| Support/Operations Team | High | service reliability | runbooks and pilot operations |

### Government / Regulatory Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Potential Role | Engagement Need |
|---|---|---|
| Sudan Medical Council | professional registration / practice rules | early regulatory consultation |
| Federal Ministry of Health | policy, legitimacy, scaling | strategic engagement |
| State Ministries of Health | local pilot permissions/coordination | before local launch |
| Sudan Medical Specialization Board | specialty/training/CPD | consult where relevant |
| Data protection/regulatory authorities in applicable jurisdictions | privacy and cross-border processing | legal/DPO-led engagement |

### Institutional / Partner Stakeholders

- hospitals and clinics;
- NGOs/humanitarian organisations;
- universities;
- professional associations;
- diaspora medical organisations;
- research institutions;
- donors/funders.

### Commercial / Technical Stakeholders

- telemedicine platform vendors;
- hosting/cloud providers;
- notification providers;
- security assessors;
- implementation partners;
- telecom/connectivity providers.

### Professional Advisory Stakeholders

- healthcare lawyers;
- privacy/DPO specialists;
- sanctions/compliance specialists;
- insurance/indemnity experts;
- tax/accounting advisers;
- public-health researchers;
- clinical informatics experts;
- cybersecurity experts.

## Stakeholder Review Rule

The stakeholder register should be reviewed:

- when pilot geography changes;
- when specialty changes;
- when a new country/jurisdiction is introduced;
- when a vendor/partner is shortlisted;
- before go-live;
- before expansion.
