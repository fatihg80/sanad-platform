# Stakeholder Communication Plan

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Goal

Ensure the right stakeholders receive the right information without exposing sensitive clinical or operational data.

## 2. Stakeholder Groups

- founding team;
- local doctors;
- consultants;
- partner facilities;
- regulators;
- NGO partners;
- funders;
- legal/privacy advisers;
- technology/vendor partners;
- research collaborators.

## 3. Communication Types

### Founding Team Review
Topics:
- decisions;
- risks;
- dependencies;
- funding;
- pilot readiness.

### Clinical Governance Review
Topics:
- incidents;
- audit;
- case quality;
- specialty workflow;
- safety.

### Operations Review
Topics:
- case volume;
- routing;
- SLA;
- support;
- connectivity;
- staffing.

### Technical Review
Topics:
- architecture;
- security;
- outages;
- releases;
- technical debt.

### Partner Update
Topics:
- progress;
- training;
- operational issues;
- evaluation.

### Funder Update
Topics:
- milestones;
- outcomes;
- budget;
- risks;
- evidence.

## 4. Confidentiality

No stakeholder update should include identifiable clinical data unless the recipient is authorised and the purpose requires it.

## 5. Escalation

Critical safety/security incidents use the incident-response process, not ordinary project communications.

## 6. Decision Records

Material decisions should be documented in Git, ADRs, or meeting decision records rather than remaining only in chat/email.
