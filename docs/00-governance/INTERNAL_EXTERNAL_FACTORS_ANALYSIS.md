# Internal and External Factors Analysis

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Active strategic context

## Purpose

Identify factors that can influence Sanad even when they are not controlled by the software team.

This analysis should inform risks, assumptions, stakeholder engagement, and major decisions.

# 1. Internal Factors

## Governance

Factors:

- clarity of founder roles;
- decision speed;
- availability of clinical leadership;
- quality of multidisciplinary collaboration;
- documentation discipline.

Potential effect:

- unclear authority can delay or weaken decisions.

## Clinical Capacity

Factors:

- number of available consultants;
- specialty mix;
- local doctor engagement;
- clinical governance maturity.

## Technical Capacity

Factors:

- engineering capability;
- security capability;
- ability to support 24/7 infrastructure even if clinical service is not 24/7;
- vendor-management capability.

## Operational Capacity

Factors:

- coordinator availability;
- support;
- training;
- onboarding;
- incident handling.

## Financial Capacity

Factors:

- grant funding;
- runway;
- consultant fees;
- staffing;
- vendor/platform cost.

## Organisational Culture

Factors:

- willingness to report incidents;
- willingness to challenge decisions;
- respect for clinical/local context;
- resistance to documentation burden.

# 2. External Factors: PESTLE View

## Political

- conflict conditions;
- government stability;
- regional control;
- neutrality perception;
- public-sector approvals;
- cross-border political relationships.

Potential impact:

- participant safety;
- partner access;
- regulatory engagement;
- hosting/payment availability.

## Economic

- funding availability;
- inflation;
- currency movement;
- payment infrastructure;
- consultant compensation;
- cloud/vendor pricing.

## Social

- trust in remote specialist advice;
- clinician adoption;
- diaspora willingness;
- patient consent expectations;
- language;
- health literacy.

## Technological

- internet availability;
- mobile-device quality;
- electricity;
- telecom reliability;
- image capture;
- cloud accessibility;
- cybersecurity threats.

## Legal / Regulatory

- medical licensing;
- telemedicine rules;
- cross-border professional practice;
- data protection;
- research ethics;
- sanctions;
- contracts;
- indemnity.

## Environmental / Physical

- conflict damage;
- power outages;
- facility disruption;
- device loss;
- physical safety of clinicians;
- displacement.

# 3. External Institutional Factors

## Governments and Regulators

Can affect:

- permission;
- professional eligibility;
- policy;
- data processing;
- scale.

## Hospitals and Facilities

Can affect:

- adoption;
- case volume;
- outcome follow-up;
- local coordination;
- data quality.

## NGOs and Humanitarian Partners

Can affect:

- access;
- funding;
- logistics;
- neutrality;
- connectivity;
- facility selection.

## Professional Associations / Diaspora Networks

Can affect:

- consultant recruitment;
- credibility;
- specialty leadership;
- professional standards.

## Technology Vendors

Can affect:

- platform control;
- security;
- cost;
- data portability;
- speed to pilot.

# 4. Monitoring Rule

Review this analysis:

- before pilot site selection;
- before platform selection;
- quarterly during pilot preparation;
- after major political/regulatory change;
- before expansion.
