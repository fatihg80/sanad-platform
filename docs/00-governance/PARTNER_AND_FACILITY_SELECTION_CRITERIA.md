# Partner and Facility Selection Criteria

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Scope:** Initial pilot partners and facilities

## 1. Purpose

Define objective criteria for selecting organisations and facilities for the first Sanad pilot.

## 2. Selection Principle

The first pilot partner should maximise:

- clinical usefulness;
- operational feasibility;
- neutrality;
- user safety;
- learning value;
- ability to complete evaluation.

The pilot should not select a site only because it is easiest to reach.

## 3. Mandatory Criteria

A facility or partner should satisfy all mandatory conditions.

### Clinical Legitimacy

- recognised healthcare facility or approved partner;
- named clinical leadership contact;
- doctors with verifiable professional status;
- willingness to follow Sanad governance rules.

### Operational Commitment

- named facility coordinator;
- commitment to onboarding and training;
- willingness to record outcomes;
- participation in incident and audit processes;
- reliable communication with Sanad operations.

### Connectivity

- at least intermittent internet access;
- realistic ability to submit structured cases;
- practical mechanism for sending required images/results.

The pilot does not require continuous high-speed connectivity.

### Neutrality and Safety

- participation does not compromise Sanad's neutrality;
- user identities and locations can be handled safely;
- facility participation does not create unacceptable physical risk.

### Evaluation

- willing to support pilot data collection;
- willing to participate in feedback;
- can provide enough cases for meaningful learning.

## 4. Preferred Criteria

- established NGO or institutional support;
- stable local administration;
- prior digital-health or teleconsultation experience;
- ability to provide basic training space/support;
- sufficient case volume in candidate specialty;
- local referral network known;
- willingness to nominate backup coordinator.

## 5. Partner Organisation Criteria

Potential NGO/institutional partners should be assessed for:

- operational presence;
- reputation and neutrality;
- clinical governance maturity;
- financial controls;
- sanctions/compliance compatibility;
- data-protection capability;
- ability to support local payments where relevant;
- ability to support connectivity/logistics;
- willingness to sign clear roles/responsibilities.

## 6. Exclusion Criteria

Do not select a partner/facility where:

- professional legitimacy cannot be verified;
- patient/clinician safety risk is unacceptable;
- the organisation demands unrestricted access to clinical data;
- political conditions would undermine neutrality;
- the facility cannot commit to follow-up;
- there is no accountable local contact;
- contractual or sanctions concerns cannot be resolved.

## 7. Assessment Areas

Suggested scoring:

| Area | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Clinical need | 5 |
| Facility commitment | 5 |
| Safety/neutrality | 5 |
| Connectivity feasibility | 4 |
| Case volume | 4 |
| Local coordination | 5 |
| Evaluation readiness | 4 |
| Partner governance | 4 |
| Regulatory feasibility | 5 |
| Data/privacy readiness | 4 |

## 8. Due Diligence Pack

Before final selection collect:

- facility/organisation identity;
- leadership contact;
- participating clinical services;
- expected doctors;
- connectivity profile;
- equipment/device profile;
- data-handling practices;
- incident escalation contact;
- proposed pilot specialty volume;
- partner legal details;
- sanctions/compliance screening where applicable.

## 9. MoU / Agreement Topics

Partner agreements should address:

- scope;
- roles;
- clinical responsibility;
- user verification;
- data protection;
- incident management;
- confidentiality;
- training;
- evaluation;
- publication/research;
- termination;
- data return/deletion;
- neutrality;
- payments where applicable.

## 10. Scope Guardrail

Partner selection must support the defined pilot.

It should not create commitments to:

- national deployment;
- additional specialties;
- direct patient telemedicine;
- exclusive long-term vendor relationships

before the pilot evidence is reviewed.
