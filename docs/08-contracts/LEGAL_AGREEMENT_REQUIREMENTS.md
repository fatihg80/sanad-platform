# Legal Agreement Requirements

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Drafting input for qualified legal counsel  
**Important:** This is not a legal contract.

## 1. Consultant Service Agreement

Should address:

- professional eligibility;
- scope of tele-expertise;
- no direct patient relationship unless legally defined otherwise;
- response expectations;
- confidentiality;
- documentation;
- clinical responsibility/liability;
- indemnity;
- conflicts of interest;
- code of conduct;
- payment;
- sanctions;
- termination;
- governing law.

## 2. Facility / Partner Agreement

Should address:

- participating facility scope;
- local doctor verification;
- local clinical responsibility;
- consent;
- data handling;
- coordinator role;
- training;
- incident escalation;
- audit cooperation;
- evaluation data;
- confidentiality;
- termination;
- service limitations.

## 3. Data Processing / Sharing Agreement

Should address:

- controller/processor roles;
- purpose;
- categories of data;
- instructions;
- security;
- subprocessors;
- international transfers;
- retention/deletion;
- breach notification;
- audit rights;
- data-subject requests.

## 4. Technology Vendor Agreement

Should address:

- services;
- hosting region;
- availability;
- security controls;
- subprocessors;
- support access;
- incident notification;
- backups;
- data ownership;
- export;
- deletion;
- portability;
- exit assistance;
- IP/customisation;
- lock-in risk.

## 5. Research Collaboration Agreement

Should address:

- protocol;
- ethics;
- dataset;
- authorship;
- publication rights;
- IP;
- data access;
- confidentiality;
- retention;
- reuse;
- termination.

## 6. Funder Agreement

Should clarify:

- restricted funding;
- reporting;
- deliverables;
- publication;
- audit;
- procurement;
- sanctions/compliance;
- IP/data expectations.

## 7. Legal Drafting Rule

Project documents may define requirements.

Only qualified legal counsel should produce/approve binding agreement language for live use.

## Required Expert Review

**Level:** Blocking before any binding agreement is signed.

**Experts:** Qualified Legal Counsel for each relevant jurisdiction, Privacy Counsel/DPO for data clauses, Indemnity/Insurance expert, Sanctions/Compliance specialist where applicable.

This repository defines requirements only; final binding language must be drafted or approved by counsel.
