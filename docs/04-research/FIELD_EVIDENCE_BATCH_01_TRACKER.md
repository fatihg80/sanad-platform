## 5. Batch Completion Criteria

Batch FE-B01 is ready for analysis when:

- at least 8 local-doctor responses are validated;
- at least 2 facility responses are validated;
- at least 4 consultant responses are validated;
- no single facility or specialty dominates the evidence without being clearly labelled;
- missing and contradictory responses are recorded rather than silently resolved.

These are working discovery thresholds and require Public Health / Evaluation review before being treated as formal research thresholds.

## 6. Processing Sequence

For every returned form:

1. confirm no patient-identifiable data is present;
2. assign/confirm the reserved Evidence ID;
3. record source and date;
4. assess evidence quality;
5. add the validated item to `EVIDENCE_INTAKE_REGISTER.md`;
6. update relevant Specialty Decision Evidence Table cells;
7. record contradictions or new risks;
8. do **not** score a specialty from one respondent alone.

## 7. Outputs from Batch 01

Expected outputs:

- first Sudan-specific local-need evidence;
- first facility feasibility evidence;
- first consultant-capacity evidence;
- evidence gaps for Batch 02;
- initial population of the weighted specialty scorecard;
- recommendation on which specialty candidates remain in the shortlist.

## 8. Decision Guardrail

Batch 01 may narrow the shortlist.

It must **not** by itself approve the pilot specialty without:

- Clinical Governance review;
- relevant specialty expert review;
- Public Health / Evaluation review;
- review of exclusion criteria.
