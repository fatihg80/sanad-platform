## Evidence Quality

### High
Documented, direct, current, and verifiable.

### Medium
Direct stakeholder evidence but approximate or incomplete.

### Low
Anecdotal, indirect, old, or not independently verifiable.

Low-quality evidence may identify a question but should not close a critical decision alone.

## Evidence Status

- Planned
- Collected
- Validated
- Superseded
- Rejected

## Rule

Every score in a specialty, facility, or vendor decision table should be traceable to one or more Evidence IDs.
