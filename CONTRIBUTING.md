# Contributing to Sanad

Sanad is a multidisciplinary project. Contributions may come from clinical, legal, public-health, research, product, security, engineering, operations, and partner stakeholders.

Read:

1. [README.md](README.md)

3. [docs/00-governance/SCOPE_GUARDRAILS.md](docs/00-governance/SCOPE_GUARDRAILS.md)

## Contribution Rule

A contribution should identify:

- the document or requirement being changed;
- the evidence or reason for the change;
- affected disciplines;
- any required expert review;
- any source or pre-existing material used.

## Authorship and IP

Contributors retain attribution for their authored contributions unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Do not submit material that you do not have authority to contribute, including employer-owned, patient-identifiable, confidential, licensed, or third-party material.

The repository's final licence, contributor IP terms, and any assignment to a future Sanad legal entity remain an explicit legal/governance decision under **ER-28 / DEC-014**. Substantial external contributions should not rely on assumptions about ownership or licensing.

## Clinical / Legal / Regulatory Contributions

A Git commit or pull request does not itself constitute clinical, legal, regulatory, privacy, or research-ethics approval. Required approvals must be recorded through the project governance process.
